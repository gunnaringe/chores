Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

This page describes how the operator's own instance of this app, running at chores.apphub.casa and ukepenger.apphub.casa, handles your data. The app itself is open source (source on GitHub, Apache License 2.0) and anyone can run their own instance instead; this policy only covers the operator's own instance and the Google Play listing that points at it.

Who this is

This app is a small, self-hosted family chores and allowance tracker, run by one person as a personal project. Contact: gunnar.inge@sort.land.

What data is collected

  • Login identity. Logging in goes through Auth0, which hands the app a name and email address for your account. This app never sees or stores your password.
  • Family data you enter. Family and child names, tasks, completion history, and allowance balances — all entered by a parent or child in the family. Money amounts are a made-up in-app allowance, not real payments; nothing here connects to a bank account or payment processor.
  • Push notification subscriptions. If you turn on notifications, your browser registers a push subscription with the server so other family members' devices can be notified when a chore is completed. Notification text (a child's name and a task title) is delivered through your browser's or device's push service (e.g. Google's for Chrome/Android, Apple's for Safari/iOS, Mozilla's for Firefox) to reach your device — the operator only ever sends to that service, and never reads what it does with it beyond delivery.
  • Session cookies. A login session is kept server-side and referenced by a cookie in your browser, so you stay logged in between visits.

Your language and theme preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage and never sent to the server.

What is not collected

There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind beyond what's listed above. Your data is never sold or shared with anyone outside your own family, other than the third parties named above that are strictly necessary to run the service (Auth0 for login, your browser's push service if you enable notifications). The app also loads the Material Symbols icon font from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers (and so Google sees your IP address) whenever a page with icons loads.

Children's data

This app is meant to be used within a family, by parents who choose what information about their children — first names and chore activity — to enter. It is not directed at children as its primary audience, and a child only appears in it because a parent or the child themself, invited into that family, put them there. If you're a parent and want a child's data removed, contact the operator using the details above.

Data retention and deletion

Detailed day-by-day task history is kept for about two months, after which it's compacted into a monthly total per child (the running balance is preserved either way). Account and family data is kept for as long as the family uses the app. To have your family's data deleted entirely, contact the operator using the details above.

Where data is hosted

The operator's instance runs on Fly.io in Stockholm, Sweden, with its database on a persistent disk attached to that same machine. Login is handled by Auth0's EU region.
